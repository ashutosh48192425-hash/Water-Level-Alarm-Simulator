A team-based electronics project designed to detect the presence of water and generate an audible alarm. The system uses a water sensor, control circuit PCB, and power supply to provide a simple warning mechanism against water leakage or overflow.

## ⚙️ Key Components
- Control Circuit PCB
- Water Sensor
- Integrated Circuits (ICs)
- Relay
- Resistors and Capacitors
- LED Indicator
- Buzzer
- Power Supply Unit

## 🔧 Working Principle
When water comes in contact with the sensor, it generates an electrical signal that is received by the control circuit. The circuit processes this signal and activates the buzzer to alert the user.

## 🎯 Applications
- Water tank overflow detection
- Water leakage alerts
- Water accumulation detection
- Domestic and industrial water monitoring

## ✨ Features
- Simple electronic design
- Audible warning system
- Cost-effective implementation
- Practical application of electronic circuits

## 📌 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how basic electronic components can be combined to build a water detection and alarm system. It also provides practical experience in PCB design, circuit connections, and electronic hardware implementation.
